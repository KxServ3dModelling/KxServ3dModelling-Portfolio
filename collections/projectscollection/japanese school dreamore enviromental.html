Project Overview

Japanese school hallway explores contrast between human architecture and a persistence of nature. Dubbed "Dreamcore" as there is little logic to the image similar to the lack of logic in dreams.

Project Breakdown

  • Created entirely in Blender.
  • Post-processing done in Davinci Resolve.
  • Rendered using Cycles. Render time: 26 Minutes.
  • Procedural materials.
  • Volumetric lighting.
  • Rendered at 4096 samples.
